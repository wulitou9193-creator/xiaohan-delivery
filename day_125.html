主题：教育与学习 · 状态：complete · 打开学习单（新窗口）

语境短文

教育的相互依存

In the modern world, education is not a solitary journey. The interdependence between students, teachers, and technology is indispensable. Each relies on the other to create a meaningful learning experience. However, this system can face challenges. For instance, a student who suffers an injury may need time to recover. During this period, schools must compensate by offering flexible deadlines or additional support. Such compensation ensures that no learner is left behind. Moreover, admission to a good university often depends on more than just grades. Diplomatic skills and the ability to work independently are highly valued. While some courses are compulsory, others allow students to explore their interests freely. In conclusion, education thrives when we recognize the indispensable role of every participant. It is not about being overweight with knowledge, but about finding the right balance. Let us cherish this interdependence and strive to learn together.

中文梗概

这篇短文讨论了现代教育中师生和学校之间的相互依存关系。它提到学生受伤时需要补偿机制，大学录取不仅看成绩，还看重独立和外交能力。最后强调平衡和合作的重要性。

interdependence UK /,ɪntədɪ'pendəns/ US /,ɪntɚdɪ'pɛndəns/

n · 相互依靠，相互依存

用法：通常作不可数名词，常与介词 with 或 between 搭配，表示两者或多者之间的相互依赖关系。

  • The interdependence of the global economy means that a crisis in one country can affect the whole world.
    全球经济的相互依存意味着一个国家的危机可能影响整个世界。
  • In a healthy relationship, there is a balance between independence and interdependence.
    在健康的关系中，独立和相互依赖之间需要平衡。

compensate UK /kɒmpenseɪt/ US /kɑmpɛnset/

v · 弥补，补偿

用法：及物动词，常用结构 compensate sb for sth（为某事补偿某人），也可用 compensate for sth（弥补某事）。

  • The company compensated the workers for their overtime.
    公司为工人的加班时间给予了补偿。
  • Nothing can compensate for the loss of a loved one.
    失去亲人，任何东西都无法弥补。

搭配：{'en': 'compensate for a loss', 'zh': '弥补损失'}、{'en': 'compensate someone for damage', 'zh': '赔偿某人的损失'}、{'en': 'compensate for a mistake', 'zh': '弥补错误'}、{'en': 'compensate for the lack of', 'zh': '弥补……的不足'}

compensation UK /ˌkɒmpenˈseɪʃn/ US /,kɑmpɛn'seʃən/

n · 补偿，赔偿；报酬

用法：作名词，常与 for 连用，表示“对……的补偿”。

  • The company offered him financial compensation for the injury.
    公司为他的受伤提供了经济补偿。
  • She received compensation for the extra hours she worked.
    她因加班得到了补偿。

搭配：{'en': 'financial compensation', 'zh': '经济补偿'}、{'en': 'compensation for damages', 'zh': '损害赔偿'}、{'en': 'claim compensation', 'zh': '索赔'}、{'en': 'compensation package', 'zh': '薪酬福利包'}

admission UK /əd'miʃən/ US /əd'mɪʃən/

n. · 承认；入场费

用法：作“承认”时，常用搭配 make an admission；作“入场费”时，常用 admission fee。

  • Her admission of guilt surprised everyone in the courtroom.
    她在法庭上承认有罪，让所有人都感到惊讶。
  • The museum charges a small admission for students.
    博物馆对学生收取少量入场费。

搭配：{'en': 'admission fee', 'zh': '入场费'}、{'en': 'admission to university', 'zh': '大学录取'}、{'en': 'make an admission', 'zh': '承认'}、{'en': 'admission of guilt', 'zh': '认罪'}

compulsory

adj. · 强制的；义务的；必修的

用法：compulsory 是形容词，常与 education、course、subject 搭配，表示“义务教育”、“必修课”。

  • Education is compulsory for all children in China.
    在中国，所有儿童都接受义务教育。
  • The school makes it compulsory for students to wear uniforms.
    学校强制要求学生穿校服。

搭配：{'en': 'compulsory education', 'zh': '义务教育'}、{'en': 'compulsory course', 'zh': '必修课'}、{'en': 'compulsory subject', 'zh': '必修科目'}、{'en': 'compulsory attendance', 'zh': '强制出勤'}

adj · 外交的

用法：注意该词的固定搭配与词性转换（待补充）。

    （翻译待补充）

    老师把 diplomatic 写在了黑板上。

搭配：diplomatic 常见搭配（待补充）

independently UK /ˌɪndɪ'pendəntlɪ/ US /,ɪndɪ'pɛndəntli/

adv · 独立地；不依赖他人地

用法：副词，常修饰动词，表示动作是独立完成的，如 work independently、think independently。

  • She learned to solve math problems independently, without asking for help.
    她学会了独立解决数学问题，不向别人求助。
  • The two experiments were conducted independently in different labs.
    这两个实验是在不同的实验室独立进行的。

indispensable UK /,ɪndɪs'pɛnsəb!/ US /ɪndɪ'spɛnsəbl/

adj · 必不可少的，不可或缺的

用法：常与介词 to 或 for 连用，表示“对…必不可少”，如 be indispensable to/for。

  • A good dictionary is indispensable for learning a foreign language.
    一本好词典对于学习外语是必不可少的。
  • The internet has become an indispensable part of our daily life.
    互联网已成为我们日常生活中不可或缺的一部分。
