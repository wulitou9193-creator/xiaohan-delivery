主题：健康与养生 · 状态：complete · 打开学习单（新窗口）

语境短文

从伤病中恢复

After months of training, Sarah suffered a knee injury. The doctor said she would need weeks of treatment and careful rest. Sarah had never been overweight and usually recovered quickly, so the long healing process surprised her. During recovery, she learned about the importance of mental strength. Pain was not only physical; it affected her mood and confidence. She also read about how bacteria can cause infections, so she kept her wound clean. The news was full of stories about a flu epidemic that had hit the city. Many people were sick with the disease. Sarah was grateful that her problem was just a temporary injury, not a serious illness. Slowly, she began to recover. She started walking, then running. She realized that health is not just the absence of disease, but a balance of body and mind.

中文梗概

莎拉在训练中膝盖受伤，需要治疗和休息。她并不超重，但身体仍需时间恢复。她体会到心理韧性的重要，也了解到细菌和传染病。最终她慢慢痊愈，并领悟到健康是身心平衡。

injury /ˈɪndʒəri/

n. · 身体或情感的损伤；受伤；损害

用法：injury 是可数名词，常与 suffer/sustain 搭配表示受伤；have an injury 也可以接受，注意用不定冠词 an injury。

  • He suffered a serious injury in the car accident.
    他在车祸中受了重伤。
  • The injury to her ankle kept her out of the game.
    她脚踝的伤让她无法参加比赛。

搭配：sustain an injury、suffer an injury、serious injury、minor injury、injury prevention

recover /rɪˈkʌvər/

v. · 恢复，康复；重新获得（体力、意识、数据等）

用法：recover 作不及物动词时用 recover from sth；作及物动词时直接跟宾语，如 recover lost data。

  • After the surgery, it took him three months to fully recover and return to his normal routine.
    手术之后，他花了三个月时间才完全康复，回归正常生活。
  • Police have recovered the stolen paintings from a warehouse outside the city.
    警方已在城外的一个仓库里找回了被盗的画作。

搭配：recover from an illness、recover quickly、fully recover、recover lost data、recover consciousness

treatment /ˈtriːtmənt/

n. · 治疗，疗法；对待，处理

用法：表示“治疗某种疾病”时，常用 treatment for + 疾病；表示“对待某人/某物”时，常用 treatment of + 对象。

  • The new treatment for cancer has shown promising results in clinical trials.
    这种新的癌症治疗方法在临床试验中显示了令人鼓舞的结果。
  • The treatment of prisoners should respect their basic human rights.
    对待囚犯的方式应尊重他们的基本人权。

搭配：medical treatment、receive treatment、undergo treatment、treatment for、effective treatment

overweight UK /ˌəʊvəˈweɪt/ US /ˌoʊvərˈweɪt/

adj. · 超重的，体重超过正常标准的

用法：常用作形容词，可作定语或表语，如 an overweight person / He is overweight。也可作动词表示'使负担过重'，但使用较少。

  • According to the latest report, nearly 30% of adults in the country are overweight.
    根据最新报告，该国近30%的成年人超重。
  • The doctor advised him to exercise regularly because he was overweight.
    医生建议他经常锻炼，因为他超重了。

搭配：be overweight、overweight people、become overweight、overweight and obesity、slightly overweight

bacteria UK /bæk'tɪərɪə/ US /bæk'tɪrɪə/

n. · 细菌（单数 bacterium）

用法：bacteria 是复数形式，单数形式为 bacterium，在正式写作中注意主谓一致。

  • The bacteria in the yogurt are beneficial for your digestive system.
    酸奶中的细菌对你的消化系统有益。
  • Wash your hands thoroughly to remove harmful bacteria from the kitchen counter.
    彻底洗手以清除厨房台面上的有害细菌。

epidemic UK /ˌepɪ'demɪk/ US /,ɛpɪ'dɛmɪk/

n./adj. · 流行病；流行性的

用法：作名词时指疾病的广泛传播，作形容词时修饰疾病或现象，常与 of 连用。

  • The government implemented strict measures to control the epidemic.
    政府实施了严格措施来控制这场流行病。
  • There is an epidemic of obesity in many developed countries.
    许多发达国家正流行肥胖症。

搭配：{'en': 'epidemic outbreak', 'zh': '流行病爆发'}、{'en': 'epidemic prevention', 'zh': '流行病预防'}、{'en': 'epidemic of flu', 'zh': '流感流行'}、{'en': 'epidemic proportions', 'zh': '流行规模'}、{'en': 'epidemic disease', 'zh': '流行病'}

mental /ˈmentl/

adj. · 心理的；精神的；脑力的

用法：常与 health、illness、state 等词搭配，描述与心理或智力相关的状态。

  • Regular exercise can improve both your physical and mental well-being.
    定期锻炼能改善你的身体和心理健康。
  • She has a strong mental attitude and never gives up easily.
    她有坚强的精神态度，从不轻易放弃。

搭配：{'en': 'mental health', 'zh': '心理健康'}、{'en': 'mental illness', 'zh': '精神疾病'}、{'en': 'mental state', 'zh': '精神状态'}、{'en': 'mental arithmetic', 'zh': '心算'}、{'en': 'mental block', 'zh': '心理障碍'}

disease UK /dɪ'ziːz/ US /dɪ'ziz/

n. · 疾病

用法：可数名词，通常指具体的疾病种类，如 heart disease（心脏病）；泛指疾病时常用单数加冠词或复数。

  • The doctor said the disease is caused by a virus.
    医生说这种疾病是由病毒引起的。
  • Regular exercise can help prevent many diseases.
    定期锻炼有助于预防许多疾病。
