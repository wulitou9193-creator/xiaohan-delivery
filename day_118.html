主题：文化与娱乐 · 状态：complete · 打开学习单（新窗口）

语境短文

城市文化节

Last weekend, our city held a vibrant cultural festival. The main goal was to encourage everyone to participate in various forms of entertainment. I heard an amusing anecdote from an elderly man about how the festival first began fifty years ago. The opening ceremony was dramatically different from last year's. This time, organizers focused on traditional architecture, guiding visitors through the historic district. They had a particular interest in showing how local transportation has changed over the decades. One of the highlights was an interactive art project. Visitors were invited to cooperate with professional painters to create a giant mural. Even children joined in, and every participant was asked to bow politely when receiving a brush from the artists. The festival also included a health awareness section. Volunteers explained how bacteria can cause disease, and why proper hygiene helps prevent epidemics. It was a wonderful reminder that staying healthy is both a physical and mental priority. Overall, the event beautifully blended culture, education, and entertainment.

中文梗概

上周末城市举办了文化节，鼓励大家参与各种娱乐活动。我听老人讲了节日起源的趣闻。开幕式与去年大不相同，重点展示传统建筑和交通变迁。还有一个互动项目，游客与艺术家合作画壁画，孩子们也参与。健康展区讲解了细菌与疾病预防，提醒人们关注身心健康。

anecdote

n. · 轶事；趣闻（短小有趣的私人或真实小故事）

用法：可数名词，元音开头所以用 an anecdote；常和 tell/share 搭配，复数 anecdotes 也很常用。

  • He loves to share funny anecdotes about his travels in South America.
    他很喜欢分享他在南美旅行时的有趣轶事。
  • The author peppered his memoir with amusing anecdotes from his childhood.
    作者在回忆录里穿插了许多童年趣闻。

搭配：tell an anecdote、share an anecdote、amusing anecdote、personal anecdote、a collection of anecdotes

dramatically UK /drə'mætɪklɪ/ US /drə'mætɪkli/

adv · 戏剧性地；显著地；引人注目地

用法：既可形容像演戏一样的夸张举止，也可修饰数字/局势的剧烈变化；高考阅读里出现频率极高，多修饰动词。

  • The number of people working from home has increased dramatically since 2020.
    自2020年以来，在家办公的人数显著增长。
  • The actress threw her hands up dramatically when she won the award.
    那位女演员得奖时夸张地举起双手。

搭配：increase dramatically、drop dramatically、change dramatically、improve dramatically、rise dramatically

participate UK /pɑ:'tɪsɪpeɪt/ US /pɑr'tɪsə'pet/

v · 参与，参加

用法：不及物动词，后接宾语时必须搭配介词 in，即 participate in sth。

  • Employees are encouraged to participate in the company's annual charity run.
    公司鼓励员工参加年度慈善跑。
  • Students who participate in extracurricular activities often develop better time-management skills.
    参加课外活动的学生往往能培养更好的时间管理能力。

搭配：participate in、actively participate、participate in a project、participate in a discussion、participate fully

particular UK /pəˈtɪkjələ(r)/ US /pɚ'tɪkjəlɚ/

adj · 特定的；特别的；挑剔的

用法：表示「对……挑剔」时与 about 搭配（如 be particular about）；表示「特定的」时多放在名词前；副词形式是 particularly，不可用 particular 直接修饰动词。

  • She's very particular about how her coffee is made—she refuses to drink it if the milk is even slightly too hot.
    她对咖啡的做法非常挑剔——牛奶哪怕稍微热一点她都不喝。
  • I want to see this particular documentary, not just any documentary about the ocean.
    我就是想看这部纪录片，不是随便什么关于海洋的片子都行。

搭配：in particular（尤其，特别）、be particular about（对……挑剔）、pay particular attention to（特别注意）、for no particular reason（没有特别的原因）、a particular case/instance（特定的情况/例子）

transportation UK /ˌtrænspɔ:ˈteɪʃn/ US /,trænspɔr'teʃən/

n · 运输；交通系统；交通运输工具（集合名词）

用法：美式英语中常指「交通运输系统/工具的总称」；作「运输」讲是不可数名词，不要加 s；作「交通工具」时可数但多以复数形式出现。

  • Public transportation in Hangzhou is so efficient that I rarely need to drive my car.
    杭州的公共交通太高效了，我几乎不需要自己开车。
  • The transportation of fresh seafood from the coast to inland cities requires refrigerated trucks.
    把新鲜海鲜从沿海运到内陆城市需要用到冷藏卡车。

搭配：public transportation、means of transportation、transportation system、transportation hub、transportation costs

cooperate UK /kəʊ'ɒpəreɪt/ US /koʊˈɑːpəreɪt/

v · 合作，协作；配合

用法：不及物动词，单独使用不接宾语，必须搭配 with sb.（和某人合作）或 on/in sth.（在某事上合作），如 cooperate with him on the project。

  • Our team decided to cooperate with the marketing department on the new product launch.
    我们团队决定和市场部合作完成这次新品发布。
  • If the weather cooperates, we'll have the outdoor wedding this Saturday.
    如果天气配合的话，这周六我们就办户外婚礼。

搭配：cooperate with sb.、cooperate on a project、cooperate to do sth.、in cooperation with、fully cooperate

architecture UK /ɑːkɪtektʃə/ US /ɑrkə'tɛktʃɚ/

n. · 建筑学；建筑设计；建筑风格

用法：作"建筑风格/建筑学"时是不可数名词（不能加 a/an）；作"某栋具体建筑的设计"时偶尔可数，但更常用 building。

  • The architecture of the Forbidden City reflects the imperial power of the Ming Dynasty.
    紫禁城的建筑风格体现了明朝的皇权。
  • She has decided to study architecture at university because she loves drawing buildings.
    她决定在大学读建筑学，因为她喜欢画建筑。

搭配：modern architecture 现代建筑、classical architecture 古典建筑、study architecture 学习建筑学、Gothic architecture 哥特式建筑、contemporary architecture 当代建筑

bow /baʊ/

v./n. · v. 鞠躬，低头；n. 弓（武器或乐器）

用法：注意发音：动词「鞠躬」和名词「弓」都读 /baʊ/；但动词「用弓拉（小提琴）」读 /boʊ/，拼写一样，发音别搞混。

  • The whole cast came on stage and bowed to the audience after the final scene.
    最后一场戏结束后，全体演员登台向观众鞠躬致意。
  • The hunter picked up his bow and arrow, took a deep breath, and aimed at the target.
    猎人拿起弓箭，深吸一口气，瞄准了靶子。

搭配：take a bow（鞠躬谢幕）、bow to sb.（向某人鞠躬；屈服于）、bow down（俯身；屈从）、bow and arrow（弓箭）、bow out（退出，告别）
