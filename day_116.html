主题：工作与职业 · 状态：complete · 打开学习单（新窗口）

语境短文

职场研讨会与新任命

Last Friday, our company organized a career workshop. In the opening presentation, the manager highlighted the value of responsibility and gave a warm affirmation to the team. However, a few employees were absent, as a flu epidemic swept through the office. The disease, which spreads via bacteria, also takes a toll on mental health. This reminded us to prioritize well-being. To strengthen the finance team, the board decided to appoint a new accountant. She impressed everyone with her detailed reports. We also launched a promotional campaign for our new product. All in all, the workshop was a success. It boosted our sense of responsibility and teamwork.

中文梗概

公司上周五举办了职业发展研讨会，经理在演示中强调责任并肯定团队。但流感疫情导致一些员工缺席，提醒大家注意由细菌传播的疾病和精神健康。为加强财务部门，董事会任命了新会计，并推出促销活动。整个研讨会增强了大家责任感与团队精神。

affirmation UK /,æfə:'meiʃən/ US /,æfɚ'meʃən/

n · 断言；肯定；正式确认

用法：作名词，常用于肯定情感或信心，也可表示法律上的正式声明。常见搭配有 make an affirmation、give affirmation、positive affirmation。

  • She nodded in affirmation, her eyes shining with pride.
    她肯定地点点头，眼里闪着骄傲的光。
  • The teacher's positive affirmation encouraged the students to keep trying.
    老师的积极肯定鼓励学生们继续尝试。

搭配：positive affirmation、self-affirmation、give affirmation、in affirmation、make an affirmation

responsibility UK /ri,spɔnsə'biliti/ US /rɪˌspɑːnsəˈbɪləti/

n · 责任；职责；责任感

用法：作不可数名词时表示“责任”的抽象概念，常搭配 take responsibility for；作可数名词时表示“具体的职责”。

  • Taking on too much responsibility at work made her feel exhausted.
    工作中承担太多责任让她感到精疲力竭。
  • He accepted full responsibility for the project's failure and resigned.
    他为项目的失败承担了全部责任，并辞职了。

搭配：take responsibility for、a sense of responsibility、social responsibility、accept responsibility、be responsible for

workshop UK /wɜːkʃɒp/ US /wɝkʃɑp/

n · 车间，工坊；（艺术/学术类的）创作室或研讨会

用法：可数名词；当「研讨会、工作坊」讲时常用 have/hold/run/attend a workshop，搭配介词 on 表示主题。

  • She runs a small ceramics workshop in the back of her garden.
    她在自家花园后面经营一间小陶艺工作室。
  • Our company held a two-day workshop on leadership skills.
    我们公司办了一场为期两天的领导力研讨会。

搭配：run a workshop、hold a workshop、attend a workshop、workshop on (topic)、hands-on workshop

promotional UK /prə'məʊʃənl/ US /prə'moʃənl/

adj · 促销的；推广的；晋升的

用法：常作定语修饰名词，如promotional campaign；它是形容词，不是名词或动词。

  • The company plans to run a promotional event next month to boost sales.
    公司计划下个月举办促销活动来提高销量。
  • Many customers received promotional emails about the summer sale.
    许多顾客收到了关于夏季打折的促销邮件。

搭配：promotional campaign、promotional material、promotional price、promotional video、promotional offer

presentation UK /ˌprezn'teɪʃn/ US /,prizɛn'teʃən/

n · 演示；报告（演讲）；外观；赠送

用法：作"报告/演讲"讲时是可数名词，常搭配 give/make/deliver；作"外观/呈现方式"讲时，侧重给人的整体感觉（如 the presentation of the dish）。

  • She gave an excellent presentation on climate change at the conference.
    她在会议上做了一个关于气候变化的精彩演讲。
  • The presentation of the new iPhone was streamed live to millions of viewers worldwide.
    新款 iPhone 的发布会向全球数百万观众进行了直播。

搭配：give a presentation、make/deliver a presentation、PowerPoint presentation、presentation skills、business presentation

absent UK /æbs(ə)nt/ US /ˈæbsənt/

adj. · 缺席的，不在场的

用法：作形容词必搭配 from（absent from work），动词重音在第二音节 /æbˈsent/，注意与 absent-minded（心不在焉）区分。

  • He was absent from the team meeting because of a sudden fever.
    他因为突然发烧没来参加团队会议。
  • Three employees have been absent from work for over a week.
    有三位员工已经一周多没来上班了。

搭配：be absent from work、absent without leave (擅离职守)、absent-minded (心不在焉的)、be absent from school、noticeably/ conspicuously absent (明显缺席)

accountant UK /ə'kauntənt/ US /ə'kaʊntənt/

n. · 会计；会计师，负责记录、核算和报告财务数据的人。

用法：这是可数名词，复数是 accountants；表示职业时用 an accountant 或 work as an accountant。

  • The accountant prepared the company's tax return before the deadline.
    会计师在截止日期前准备好了公司的纳税申报表。
  • After reviewing the receipts, our accountant found several expenses that should not be claimed.
    检查收据后，我们的会计发现有几项费用不应该申报。

搭配：hire an accountant、work as an accountant、a certified public accountant、a chartered accountant、a tax accountant

appoint UK /ə'pɔint/ US /ə'pɔɪnt/

v. · 任命;委派;约定(时间/地点)

用法：及物动词,常用于 appoint sb as/to sth 或 appoint sb to do sth,常以被动语态 be appointed 出现在考试中。

  • The board appointed her as the new chief executive officer last month.
    上个月董事会任命她为新的首席执行官。
  • Let's appoint a time to meet and discuss the project in detail.
    我们定个时间碰面,详细聊聊这个项目吧。

搭配：appoint sb as...、appoint sb to a position、be appointed to do sth、newly appointed director、appoint a date/time
