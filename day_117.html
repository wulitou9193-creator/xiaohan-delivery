语境短文

友谊与康复

Mark suffered a serious injury. He felt hopeless and thought his life was over. His friends, however, did not abandon him. They visited every day and offered their full support. Lucy, a close friend, was passionate about fitness. She created a gentle treatment plan to help him recover. Mark was overweight and insecure, but Lucy never made him feel ashamed. She was affectionate yet firm with him. Her warm personality gave him strength. Mark and his teammates were alike in their determination to see him heal. Sometimes, so much attention began to annoy him. But deep down, he appreciated their care. Their affection was a medicine stronger than any drug. Slowly, Mark learned to walk again. He realized that recovery is not just physical; it is also emotional. Now he supports others in the same situation. He knows that with love and patience, anyone can overcome an injury.

中文梗概

马克受伤后心灰意冷，朋友们没有抛弃他，每天陪伴并给予全力支持。好友露西热爱健身，为他制定了温和的恢复计划，尽管他超重且不自信，露西却从不让难堪，对他温柔又不失严格。她的温暖个性和队友们相似的决心给了他力量，虽然过度的关心有时让他烦躁，但他最终康复，并领悟到爱与陪伴的力量。

support /səˈpɔːrt/

v./n. · 支持；支撑；帮助或赞同

用法：support 作及物动词时常接名词、代词或动名词；说“支持某人做某事”用 support someone in doing something。

  • My family always supports my decisions.
    我的家人总是支持我的决定。
  • The steel cables support the glass roof above the main hall.
    钢索支撑着大厅上方的玻璃屋顶。

搭配：support a decision、support a cause、support a family、provide support to someone、in support of

passionate UK /ˈpæʃənət/ US /pæʃənət/

adj · 充满激情的；热烈的；热烈的（情感上强烈投入的）

用法：可形容人也可形容事；表示'对…充满热情'时固定搭配 be passionate about sth（介词用 about，不用 with/in）。

  • Lena is passionate about marine biology and spends every summer diving to study coral reefs.
    Lena 对海洋生物学充满热情，每年夏天都潜水研究珊瑚礁。
  • His passionate defence of free speech made him both admired and controversial on campus.
    他为言论自由所做的激烈辩护让他在校园里既受人敬佩也备受争议。

搭配：be passionate about sth、a passionate kiss、a passionate debate、a passionate advocate (of sth)、passionate love

affectionate UK /ə'fekʃ(ə)nət/ US /ə'fɛkʃənət/

adj · 深情的；柔情表达的（侧重外显的动作或态度）

用法：通常用来形容人或动物「表达感情的方式」，如拥抱、亲吻、目光、信件；不形容内在状态（如心情）。

  • She sent her son an affectionate letter every week while he was studying abroad.
    儿子在国外念书的那几年，她每周都寄去一封充满深情的信。
  • The old golden retriever gave an affectionate lick on the little girl's hand.
    那只老金毛深情地舔了舔小女孩的手。

搭配：an affectionate hug、an affectionate kiss、an affectionate letter、an affectionate nature、affectionate toward sb

personality UK /ˌpɜ:səˈnæləti/ US /,pɝsə'næləti/

n · 个性；性格；人格特质

用法：personality 表示笼统的“个性”时通常作不可数名词；表示某人的一种具体性格时，常说 a cheerful personality。

  • Her cheerful personality helps her make friends quickly whenever she joins a new class.
    每当进入一个新班级，她开朗的性格都能帮助她迅速交到朋友。
  • Online interviews can reveal a candidate's personality when their answers feel genuine and relaxed.
    当求职者的回答真诚又放松时，视频面试也能让人看出他的性格。

搭配：a warm personality、personality trait、personality clash、a distinct personality、part of someone's personality

abandon UK /ə'bænd(ə)n/ US /ə'bændən/

v. · 放弃；遗弃；抛弃；离弃（人、地方、责任）

用法：及物动词，后面必须直接跟宾语；表示「放弃做某事」用 abandon doing；abandon oneself to sth. 表示「沉溺于、放纵于」负面情绪。

  • After three failed startups, he finally abandoned his dream of becoming a tech founder and took a steady office job.
    在三次创业失败后，他终于放弃了当科技公司创始人的梦想，接受了稳定的办公室工作。
  • The couple were convicted of abandoning their newborn baby in a cardboard box outside a hospital.
    那对夫妇被判遗弃新生儿罪，他们把孩子丢在医院外的纸箱里。

搭配：abandon hope (放弃希望)、abandon ship (弃船逃生)、abandon oneself to despair (陷入绝望)、with abandon (放纵地；尽情地)、abandon a project / plan (放弃项目/计划)

affection UK /ə'fekʃ(ə)n/ US /ə'fɛkʃən/

n. · 爱；爱慕；深情（一种温柔、稳定、长期的喜爱）

用法：作不可数名词为主，搭配 show / have / feel affection for sb（介词用 for 不用 to）。

  • She has always shown deep affection for her younger brother, even after he moved abroad.
    她一直很疼爱弟弟，即使他搬去国外后也是如此。
  • The affection between the two old friends was obvious to everyone in the room.
    在场所有人都看得出这两位老友之间的深厚情谊。

搭配：show affection for sb、feel affection for sb、win sb's affection、mutual affection、deep / genuine affection

alike /ə'laɪk/

adj./adv. · 相似的（地）；相像的（地）

用法：alike 通常只作表语或宾语补足语，不作定语修饰名词；作副词时常放在动词后或句末。

  • The two brothers look so alike that their teachers often mix them up in class.
    这兄弟俩长得太像，老师在课上经常把他们搞混。
  • Managers and employees don't always think alike when it comes to company policy.
    在涉及公司政策时，管理者和员工的看法不一定相同。

搭配：look alike、think alike、dress alike、alike in appearance、share alike

annoy UK /ə'nɒɪ/ US /ə'nɔɪ/

v. · 使烦恼；打扰；使恼怒

用法：及物动词，后直接接人或物；常用被动结构 be annoyed at/with/about sth.

  • The constant buzzing of the phone annoyed me during the meeting.
    会议期间手机持续的嗡嗡声让我心烦。
  • She was annoyed with herself for forgetting the key.
    她因为忘了钥匙而生自己的气。

搭配：annoy someone、be annoyed at、be annoyed with、be annoyed about、annoying habit

复习词

  • injury /ˈɪndʒəri/ — 身体或情感的损伤；受伤；损害
  • recover /rɪˈkʌvər/ — 恢复，康复；重新获得（体力、意识、数据等）
  • treatment /ˈtriːtmənt/ — 治疗，疗法；对待，处理
  • overweight UK /ˌəʊvəˈweɪt/ US /ˌoʊvərˈweɪt/ — 超重的，体重超过正常标准的
