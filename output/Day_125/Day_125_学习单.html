评分 = 揭晓答案 + 展开输入内容

再输入

interdependence

nUK /,ɪntədɪ'pendəns/ US /,ɪntɚdɪ'pɛndəns/

相互依靠，相互依存

📖 词根拆解

这个词由拉丁语前缀 inter-（在……之间）和 dependere（悬挂、依赖）组合而成。dependere 由 de-（向下）和 pendere（悬挂）构成，字面意思是“挂在下面”，引申为“依靠”。所以 interdependence 字面意思就是“彼此挂在对方下面”，即相互依靠。

🧠 记忆法
  • 词根拆分法：inter（之间）+ depend（依赖）+ ence（名词后缀）→ 彼此依赖 → 相互依靠。
  • 场景联想法：想象两个登山者用绳子系在一起，一个滑倒另一个拉住，他们相互依靠才能生存。
  • 对比记忆法：对比 dependence（依赖）和 independence（独立），inter- 表示“相互”，所以 interdependence 是“相互依赖”，强调双向关系。
💡 用法

通常作不可数名词，常与介词 with 或 between 搭配，表示两者或多者之间的相互依赖关系。

⚠️ 易错点
  • ❌ The interdependence of the two countries are important.
    ✅ The interdependence of the two countries is important.
    interdependence 是不可数名词，谓语动词用单数。
  • ❌ They have a strong interdependence on each other.
    ✅ They have a strong interdependence with each other.
    表示“与……相互依存”时，介词用 with 而不是 on。
🎯 考点
  • 在阅读理解中，常出现于经济、生态、社会类文章，考查对相互依存关系的理解。
  • 在写作中，可用于论述全球化、合作等话题，注意与 dependence 区分。
🌳 词族

🔗 常见搭配

✏️ 真实例句
  • The interdependence of the global economy means that a crisis in one country can affect the whole world.
    全球经济的相互依存意味着一个国家的危机可能影响整个世界。
  • In a healthy relationship, there is a balance between independence and interdependence.
    在健康的关系中，独立和相互依赖之间需要平衡。
🔍 近义辨析
  • interdependence（相互依赖）— 强调双向、相互的依赖关系。
  • dependence（依赖）— 强调单向的依赖，不强调相互性。

💡 用法

    教育的相互依存

    In the modern world, education is not a solitary journey. The interdependence between students, teachers, and technology is indispensable. Each relies on the other to create a meaningful learning experience.

    However, this system can face challenges. For instance, a student who suffers an injury may need time to recover. During this period, schools must compensate by offering flexible deadlines or additional support. Such compensation ensures that no learner is left behind.

    Moreover, admission to a good university often depends on more than just grades. Diplomatic skills and the ability to work independently are highly valued. While some courses are compulsory, others allow students to explore their interests freely.

    In conclusion, education thrives when we recognize the indispensable role of every participant. It is not about being overweight with knowledge, but about finding the right balance. Let us cherish this interdependence and strive to learn together.

    中文梗概

    这篇短文讨论了现代教育中师生和学校之间的相互依存关系。它提到学生受伤时需要补偿机制，大学录取不仅看成绩，还看重独立和外交能力。最后强调平衡和合作的重要性。
