语境短文

牙痛背后的真相：压力如何影响你的健康

A toothache can be more than just pain. It might be a sign that your body is fundamentally stressed. When we feel insecure about our health, our mental state suffers, and this can influence our physical condition. Stress is a powerful influencer. It can change how our body fights bacteria and disease. An influential study found that chronic stress can even affect our teeth. So, instead of ignoring a toothache, we should address it patiently. Our body's headquarters, the brain, sends signals under stress. This can make us feel insecure and anxious. But remember, we can influence our health positively. By managing stress, we can prevent a toothache from becoming an epidemic of poor health.

中文梗概

牙痛不仅仅是小问题，它可能是身体压力的信号。压力会影响我们的心理健康和身体抵抗力，甚至改变口腔细菌的平衡。我们应耐心对待牙痛，因为大脑作为身体的总部，在压力下会让我们感到不安。学会管理压力，就能积极影响健康，防止小问题演变成大流行病。

fundamentally /ˌfʌndəˈmentəli/

adv. · 从根本上；本质上

用法：副词，常修饰动词或整个句子，表示“从根本上”或“本质上”，常用于正式或学术语境。

  • The new policy has fundamentally changed the way we work.
    新政策从根本上改变了我们的工作方式。
  • Their approach to health is fundamentally different from ours.
    他们的健康理念与我们的有本质不同。

stress /stres/

n./v. · 压力；紧张；重音；强调

用法：作名词时常用不可数，但表示'某种压力'时可加 a；作动词时意为'强调'或'使焦虑'。

  • Chronic stress can weaken the immune system over time.
    长期压力会逐渐削弱免疫系统。
  • She stressed the importance of a balanced diet.
    她强调了均衡饮食的重要性。

搭配：{'en': 'under stress', 'zh': '处于压力之下'}、{'en': 'stress management', 'zh': '压力管理'}、{'en': 'reduce stress', 'zh': '减轻压力'}、{'en': 'stress the importance', 'zh': '强调重要性'}、{'en': 'stress test', 'zh': '压力测试'}

influence UK /ɪnflʊəns/ US /ɪnfluəns/

v & n · 影响；影响力

用法：作动词时是及物动词，直接接宾语；作名词时常用 have an influence on... 结构。

  • Social media has a huge influence on how teenagers perceive themselves.
    社交媒体对青少年如何看待自己有着巨大的影响。
  • Her teacher's encouragement influenced her decision to study medicine.
    老师的鼓励影响了她学医的决定。

搭配：{'en': 'have an influence on', 'zh': '对……有影响'}、{'en': 'under the influence of', 'zh': '在……的影响下'}、{'en': 'positive/negative influence', 'zh': '积极/消极影响'}、{'en': "influence someone's decision", 'zh': '影响某人的决定'}

influential UK /ˌɪnfluˈenʃl/ US /,ɪnflu'ɛnʃl/

adj · 有影响力的，有权势的

用法：常指人或事物对他人、社会、决策等有重大影响，可用于人或抽象事物。

  • The influential scientist's research changed the way we think about climate change.
    这位有影响力的科学家的研究改变了我们对气候变化的看法。
  • She is one of the most influential figures in the fashion industry.
    她是时尚界最有影响力的人物之一。

搭配：{'en': 'influential figure', 'zh': '有影响力的人物'}、{'en': 'influential role', 'zh': '有影响力的角色'}、{'en': 'influential in', 'zh': '在…方面有影响力'}、{'en': 'highly influential', 'zh': '极具影响力的'}

patiently UK /peɪʃntlɪ/ US /peʃəntli/

adv · 耐心地，不急躁地

用法：副词，修饰动词，常与 wait, listen, explain 等动词搭配。

  • She waited patiently for the bus in the rain.
    她在雨中耐心地等公交车。
  • The teacher patiently explained the grammar rule again.
    老师耐心地又解释了一遍语法规则。

搭配：{'en': 'wait patiently', 'zh': '耐心等待'}、{'en': 'listen patiently', 'zh': '耐心倾听'}、{'en': 'explain patiently', 'zh': '耐心解释'}、{'en': 'work patiently', 'zh': '耐心工作'}、{'en': 'deal with patiently', 'zh': '耐心处理'}

toothache UK /ˈtu:θeɪk/ US /tuθek/

n · 牙痛，牙疼

用法：通常作可数名词，但常以单数形式出现，如 'have a toothache'。

  • I had a terrible toothache last night and couldn't sleep.
    我昨晚牙疼得厉害，睡不着觉。
  • She went to the dentist because of a persistent toothache.
    她因为持续牙痛去看牙医。

搭配：{'en': 'have a toothache', 'zh': '牙疼'}、{'en': 'suffer from a toothache', 'zh': '遭受牙痛'}、{'en': 'a severe toothache', 'zh': '严重的牙痛'}、{'en': 'toothache pain', 'zh': '牙痛疼痛'}

insecure UK /,ɪnsɪ'kjʊə;/ US /,ɪnsɪ'kjʊr/

adj · 不安全的；缺乏安全感的

用法：insecure 既可指物理上的不安全，也可指心理上的缺乏自信、不安。作表语时常用 be insecure about sth。

  • She felt insecure about her job after the company announced layoffs.
    公司宣布裁员后，她对工作感到不安。
  • The old bridge is structurally insecure and needs to be repaired.
    这座旧桥结构不安全，需要维修。

搭配：{'en': 'feel insecure', 'zh': '感到不安全'}、{'en': 'insecure about', 'zh': '对……缺乏安全感'}、{'en': 'emotionally insecure', 'zh': '情感上缺乏安全感'}、{'en': 'insecure attachment', 'zh': '不安全依恋'}

headquarter UK /hedkwɔːtə/ US /hɛd'kwɔrtɚ/

v · 设立总部，在…设立总部

用法：headquarter 作动词时，常用被动语态 be headquartered in/at，表示“总部设在某地”。

  • The company is headquartered in New York.
    这家公司的总部设在纽约。
  • They decided to headquarter the new division in Singapore.
    他们决定将新部门的总部设在新加坡。
