语境短文

治愈系健身 App 的诞生

Last spring, a young programmer named Lin quit her office job to build a fitness app. Her goal was simple: help people who had suffered an injury recover faster at home. She hoped her tools could accelerate the healing process. At first, results were disappointing. The app crashed often, and the directory of exercises was too short. "We only had approximately fifty users in the first month," Lin admitted. Then she added a smart feature that could compile a daily plan for each user. The app learned what treatment worked best for someone who was overweight or in pain. People said the friendly design made exercising more appealing, not boring. Now, six months later, the app has over two million users. Doctors even recommend it. "I feel deep appreciation," Lin said. "We just wanted to help."

中文梗概

程序员小林辞职开发了一款健身应用，希望帮助受伤的人在家更快康复。初期用户寥寥无几，结果令人失望，但她后来加入了智能功能，能为超重或疼痛的用户量身定制每日治疗方案。如今这款应用用户突破两百万，她对此充满感激。

programmer UK /prəʊgræmə/ US /progræmɚ/

n · 程序员；编程人员

用法：可数名词，-er 是表示'做某事的人'的施动者后缀；口语色彩比 software engineer 更浓，专指'写代码的人'。

  • My sister works as a programmer at a fast-growing tech company in Shenzhen.
    我妹妹在深圳一家发展很快的科技公司当程序员。
  • The startup is hiring experienced programmers to help build its new AI product.
    这家初创公司正在招有经验的程序员，帮他们做新的 AI 产品。

搭配：senior programmer 高级程序员、experienced programmer 经验丰富的程序员、freelance programmer 自由职业程序员、lead programmer 主程序员、programmer analyst 程序员分析师

disappointing UK /dɪsə'pɒɪntɪŋ/ US /,dɪsə'pɔɪntɪŋ/

adj · 令人失望的；让人扫兴的

用法：修饰「事/物/结果」让人觉得失望，主语通常是无生命名词或 it；形容「人感到失望」要用被动式 disappointed，不可混用。

  • The new phone's battery life is disappointing — it dies in just four hours.
    这部新手机续航太让人失望了，只能撑四个小时就没电。
  • It's disappointing that the restaurant we booked was closed when we arrived.
    我们订的那家餐厅到了才发现没开门，真扫兴。

搭配：deeply disappointing 非常令人失望的、rather disappointing 相当令人失望的、a disappointing result 令人失望的结果、a disappointing performance 表现不佳、disappointing news 令人沮丧的消息

appreciation UK /ə,pri:ʃi'eiʃən/ US /ə,priʃɪ'eʃən/

n · 感激；欣赏，鉴赏；（货币、资产等的）升值，增值

用法：短语搭配上区分：appreciation for sb/sth 表示'对…的感激/欣赏'，in appreciation of 表示'作为对…的感谢'；注意动词 appreciate 后面接 doing 而不接 to do。

  • She sent me a heartfelt card to express her appreciation for helping her move last weekend.
    她给我寄来一张真心的卡片，感谢我上周末帮她搬家。
  • The sudden appreciation of the company's shares made early investors quite wealthy overnight.
    这家公司股价突然大涨，让早期投资者一夜之间变得相当富有。

搭配：express / show appreciation (表达感激)、deep / sincere / heartfelt appreciation (深深的感激)、in appreciation of (作为对…的感谢)、appreciation for music / art / nature (对音乐/艺术/自然的欣赏)、mutual appreciation (相互欣赏)

approximately UK /ə'prɔksimitli/ US /ə'prɑksɪmətli/

adv · 大约，大概（用于数字、时间、距离等具体数值前）

用法：副词，直接放在数字/数量前使用，写作中比 about 更正式，常用于科技、学术、报告类文本。

  • The flight from Shanghai to Tokyo takes approximately three hours.
    从上海飞往东京的航班大约需要三个小时。
  • The new model costs approximately 800 dollars, which is much cheaper than the previous one.
    新款大约 800 美元，比上一代便宜不少。

搭配：approximately equal to 大约等于、approximately the same 大致相同、at approximately 大约在（某时/某量）、approximately speaking 大体说来、approximately 30 / 50% / 100 people 约 30 人 / 50% / 100 人

compile /kəm'paɪl/

v. · 编辑；编纂；汇编（计算机语境下指编译代码）

用法：日常用法强调「收集散乱材料并整理成册」，计算机语境特指把源代码翻译成可执行程序，是及物动词，直接接宾语

  • It took her two years to compile enough data for her thesis.
    她花了两年才收集到足够写论文的数据。
  • The new app failed to compile, so we couldn't test it on the phone.
    这个新应用编译失败，所以我们没法在手机上测试它。

搭配：compile a list、compile a report、compile data、compile an anthology、compile code

directory

n. · 目录；指南；（尤指）电话簿/姓名地址录

用法：可数名词，常与 phone/business/online 搭配；计算机语境里也指「文件夹」。

  • I couldn't find her number in the phone directory, so I asked directory assistance for help.
    我在电话簿里找不到她的号码，就打电话问了查询台。
  • The hotel provides a directory of nearby restaurants and tourist attractions at the front desk.
    酒店前台提供一份附近餐馆和景点的指南册。

搭配：phone directory 电话簿、business directory 商业名录、directory assistance 电话查询台、online directory 在线目录、directory listing 目录列表

accelerate UK /ək'seləreɪt/ US /əkˈsɛləˌret/

v. · 加速；促进（使某事的发生速度变快）

用法：及物与不及物皆可；表示'急剧加速'时常用 accelerate rapidly / dramatically，不要画蛇添足再加 fast。

  • The pandemic accelerated the shift toward remote work across many industries.
    疫情加速了众多行业向远程办公的转变。
  • Tesla plans to accelerate the production of its more affordable EV model next year.
    特斯拉计划明年加快更亲民款电动车的生产。

搭配：accelerate the pace of something、accelerate growth / development、accelerate the process、rapidly accelerate、accelerate progress

appealing UK /əˈpi:lɪŋ/ US /əˈpiːlɪŋ/

adj · 吸引人的；动人的；令人心动的

用法：既可形容具体事物（外观、价格、设计）吸引人，也可形容抽象事物（想法、请求）让人想接受，常与 to 搭配。

  • The travel package was appealing because it included free hotel upgrades.
    这个旅行套餐很诱人，因为包含了免费酒店升级。
  • She gave him an appealing look when she wanted to borrow his jacket.
    她想要借他的夹克时，露出了一副惹人怜的表情。

搭配：appealing to sb、find sth appealing、an appealing offer / price、visually appealing、appeal to / appeal for
