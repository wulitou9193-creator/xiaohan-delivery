主题：运动与健身 · 状态：complete · 打开学习单（新窗口）

语境短文

运动中的多样性与坚持

The community center celebrates the diversity of sports available to everyone, from intense training to casual fun. An athletic boxer spends hours practicing boxing techniques, knowing that one wrong move could lead to a serious injury. Meanwhile, others prefer the relaxed pace of bowling or the quick reflexes needed for badminton, finding joy in different rhythms. From a medical standpoint, proper treatment helps athletes recover quickly, proving that rest is not pointless but essential for long-term health. Whether you are overweight or fit, finding your niche in sports brings confidence and community spirit to daily life.

中文梗概

社区中心展示了多样化的运动项目，满足不同人群的需求。拳击手刻苦训练以防受伤，而其他人则享受保龄球或羽毛球的乐趣。正确的治疗和休息对恢复至关重要，无论体型如何，运动都能带来自信。

athletic 降级内容

adj. · 运动的；运动员的

用法：注意该词的固定搭配与词性转换（待补充）。

  • Please remember the word athletic.
    （翻译待补充）
  • The teacher wrote athletic on the board.
    老师把 athletic 写在了黑板上。

搭配：athletic 常见搭配（待补充）

boxer

n. · 拳击手

用法：专指从事拳击运动的人（职业或业余），搭配时多用 professional boxer、heavyweight boxer；注意 boxer shorts 是「平角内裤」的意思，跟拳击无关。

  • Muhammad Ali is widely regarded as the greatest boxer in history.
    穆罕默德·阿里被广泛认为是历史上最伟大的拳击手。
  • The young boxer spent six hours a day training for the championship match.
    这位年轻拳击手每天训练六小时，为冠军赛做准备。

搭配：professional boxer、heavyweight boxer、former boxer、world champion boxer、boxer shorts（平角内裤）

boxing

n. · 拳击（运动）

用法：不可数名词，指运动项目；与动词 box（打拳）搭配，注意运动前用 do，不用 play。

  • She has been training for boxing at the local gym since she was thirteen.
    她从十三岁起就在当地的健身房练拳击。
  • Boxing requires not only physical strength but also sharp reflexes and mental discipline.
    拳击不仅要求体力，还需要灵活的反应和强大的心理素质。

搭配：boxing match、boxing ring、boxing gloves、professional boxing、amateur boxing

bowling

n. · 保龄球运动

用法：作不可数名词，常用搭配 go bowling（去打保龄球），不能说 play bowling。

  • We went bowling with my coworkers last Friday and had a great time.
    上周五我们和同事一起去打保龄球，玩得很开心。
  • The bowling alley near our campus offers a student discount every Sunday.
    我们学校附近的保龄球馆每周日都给学生打折。

搭配：go bowling、bowling alley、bowling ball、bowling pin、ten-pin bowling

diversity UK /dai'və:səti/ US /dɪˈvəsɪti/

n · 多样性；差异；多元

用法：不可数名词，常用搭配 diversity of sth（某事物的多样性），写作中 biodiversity（生物多样性）和 cultural diversity（文化多样性）是高频考点。

  • Our company values diversity and hires people from over twenty different countries.
    我们公司重视多元化，雇用了来自二十多个不同国家的员工。
  • The documentary highlighted the amazing diversity of wildlife in the Amazon rainforest.
    这部纪录片着重呈现了亚马逊雨林里令人惊叹的野生动物多样性。

搭配：cultural diversity 文化多样性、biodiversity 生物多样性、diversity of opinions 观点的多样性、embrace diversity 拥抱多元、genetic diversity 遗传多样性

pointless UK /pɒɪntlɪs/ US /pɔɪntləs/

adj · 无意义的；徒劳的；无目的的

用法：形容词，常用于 It is pointless doing/to do sth，或 a pointless argument/exercise 等搭配，表否定评价。

  • Continuing to argue with the referee during the match is completely pointless — the decision won't be changed.
    比赛时还在跟裁判争论完全没意义——判罚不会因此改变。
  • He finally realized that doing endless crunches every day was pointless if he wasn't fixing his diet.
    他终于意识到，如果饮食不调整，每天做再多的仰卧起坐也是白搭。

搭配：a pointless argument、pointless to do / pointless doing、absolutely / totally pointless、a pointless exercise / drill、a pointless waste of time

standpoint UK /ˈstændpɔɪnt/ US /stændpɔɪnt/

n · 立场；观点；角度（多指带价值观或态度的看法）

用法：正式书面语，常用搭配 from the / one's standpoint（从……的角度/立场看），介词 of 前必须有 the，不能省略。

  • From a coach's standpoint, daily warm-up is just as important as the workout itself.
    在教练看来，日常热身和正式训练本身一样重要。
  • From the standpoint of long-term health, a 10-minute jog every day beats an occasional marathon.
    从长期健康的角度看，每天慢跑10分钟比偶尔跑一次马拉松更有效。

搭配：from the standpoint of、from a practical standpoint、from an economic standpoint、maintain a standpoint、shift one's standpoint

badminton 降级内容 UK /bædmɪnt(ə)n/ US /bædmɪntən/

n · 羽毛球

用法：注意该词的固定搭配与词性转换（待补充）。

  • Please remember the word badminton.
    （翻译待补充）
  • The teacher wrote badminton on the board.
    老师把 badminton 写在了黑板上。

搭配：badminton 常见搭配（待补充）
