语境短文

悬崖上的微生物警报

The research station sat on a cliff overlooking the cold sea. The air was chilly and the walls felt damp, but the team had sophisticated equipment to analyze every sample. Dr. Elena, a passionate advocator for ocean health, saw an indication that something was wrong. Unusual bacteria were found breeding near the shore. She feared a new disease could trigger an epidemic, affecting both sea life and humans. She created a catalogue of all the microbes collected from the damp rocks. Each breed was photographed and tested. The data suggested that the bacteria had mutated in the cold water. "This is a mental challenge," she admitted, "but we must stay focused." Her work revealed that the strange bacteria came from a particular coral. She reported her findings, and other scientists praised her careful research. Thanks to her quick response, the threat was contained before the disease could spread.

中文梗概

在寒冷潮湿的悬崖研究站，科学家发现靠近海岸的异常细菌，担心会引发新疾病并导致流行病。他们仔细收集样本、编号入册，发现细菌源于一种特殊珊瑚。最终及时控制住威胁，避免疫情扩散。

sophisticated /səˈfɪstɪkeɪtɪd/

adj. · 复杂的；精密的；老练的；有教养的

用法：褒义为主，形容技术/设备时强调'精密高级'，形容人时强调'品味与世故'，但形容人有时暗含'过于世故、不再单纯'。

  • Hospitals now rely on sophisticated imaging equipment to diagnose diseases earlier.
    如今的医院依赖精密的成像设备来更早地发现疾病。
  • He has a sophisticated palate and can pick out subtle flavors in a single sip of coffee.
    他的味蕾很老练，喝一口咖啡就能分辨出细微的风味差别。

搭配：sophisticated technology/equipment、sophisticated analysis、a sophisticated gentleman/lady、sophisticated taste、highly sophisticated system

advocator /ædvəkeɪtə/

n · 倡导者；主张者；拥护者（公开为某事或某人奔走发声的人）

用法：现代英语中名词更常用 advocate，advocator 偏书面、正式语体，强调"主动倡议并公开表态"的人，与 supporter(仅支持)有区别。

  • She has been a vocal advocator of renewable energy for more than twenty years.
    二十多年来，她一直是一位为可再生能源大声疾呼的倡导者。
  • As an advocator for marine conservation, he organizes beach clean-ups every weekend.
    作为海洋保护的倡导者，他每个周末都组织海滩清洁活动。

搭配：advocator of/for peace、environmental advocator、passionate advocator、outspoken advocator、advocator group

indication UK /ɪndɪ'keɪʃ(ə)n/ US /,ɪndɪ'keʃən/

n · 指示；迹象；表明

用法：抽象不可数名词，常用搭配 give an indication of / be an indication that...，后接 that 从句表示‘…的迹象’。

  • The dark clouds and the sudden drop in temperature are a clear indication that a storm is coming.
    乌云密布加上气温骤降，这是暴风雨要来的明显迹象。
  • She gave no indication of being upset, even though her voice trembled slightly.
    她没有丝毫不开心的表示，尽管声音微微发颤。

搭配：a clear indication of、give (an) indication of、strong indication that...、every indication of、be an indication that...

catalogue UK /ˈkætəlɒg/ US /kætəlɔɡ/

v · 把……编入目录；为……编目

用法：作动词时是及物动词，直接接宾语，如catalogue sth；美式拼写常用catalog。

  • The library catalogued all the donated books within a week.
    图书馆在一周内把所有捐赠的图书编入了目录。
  • Biologists have catalogued more than 10,000 species in this rainforest.
    生物学家已为这片雨林中的一万多个物种编了目。

搭配：catalogue books、catalogue a collection、catalogue data、catalogue species、catalogue items

breed UK /briːd/ US /brid/

v./n. · v. 繁殖；养育；滋生 n. (动物)品种；种类

用法：动词过去式与过去分词都是 bred（不规则），千万别加 -ed；名词指动物品种时是可数名词，常说 a breed of + 动物。

  • The farmer breeds cattle on his organic farm in the countryside.
    这个农民在他的乡村有机农场里饲养牛。
  • This rare breed of dog originated in the mountains of Tibet.
    这种稀有犬种起源于西藏的山区。

搭配：breed rapidly（快速繁殖）、a rare breed of（稀有品种）、pure breed / cross breed（纯种/杂交品种）、breed contempt for（对…产生蔑视）、breed like rabbits（像兔子一样大量繁殖）

chilly

adj. · 寒冷的；冷飕飕的（既指天气，也可指气氛冷淡）

用法：比 cold 程度略轻、更口语化；除了形容天气，还能形容「人际氛围冷淡」（a chilly welcome）。

  • It was a chilly morning, so I pulled on a sweater before heading out.
    那天早上冷飕飕的，我出门前顺手套了件毛衣。
  • The new neighbor gave us a rather chilly greeting and went straight back inside.
    新邻居跟我们打了个特别冷淡的招呼，转身就回屋了。

搭配：chilly weather、a chilly morning、a chilly wind、feel chilly、a chilly reception

cliff /klɪf/

n. · 悬崖；峭壁（多指海边或山边的陡峭岩壁）

用法：可数名词，主要指垂直或近乎垂直的岩石面；常与 edge、face 搭配；注意介词搭配 fall off a cliff（从悬崖上掉下），不是 fall from。

  • The old lighthouse stands on the top of a white cliff facing the Atlantic Ocean.
    那座古老的灯塔矗立在一面朝向大西洋的白色悬崖顶上。
  • Don't get too close to the cliff edge — the rocks have been crumbling for years.
    别太靠近悬崖边缘，那些岩石已经风化崩塌好多年了。

搭配：cliff edge、cliff face、sheer cliff、sea cliff、dwellings on a cliff

damp /dæmp/

adj. · 潮湿的，微湿的（通常指令人不快或不适的潮湿）

用法：作形容词时既可修饰具体事物（如墙、衣服），也可修饰抽象概念（如气氛）；作名词时指“潮湿、潮气”。

  • The cottage felt cold and damp, so we decided to light a fire.
    小屋又冷又潮，于是我们决定生火。
  • Wipe the surface with a damp cloth to remove the dust.
    用湿布擦一下表面去除灰尘。

搭配：damp cloth、damp weather、damp atmosphere、damp walls、damp condition
