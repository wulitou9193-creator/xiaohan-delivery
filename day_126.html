语境短文

干旱中的生命奇迹

The delicate balance of nature is often tested by drought. In the vast canyon, a mother bear and her calf searched for water. The once peaceful riverbed was now dry, and the bay beyond shimmered with heat. A lone beast, a wild horse, stood by the edge, its eyes reflecting a quiet fascination with the changing world. It seemed to correspond with the land's silent struggle. The blossoms of the desert plants, usually a sign of hope, had not yet appeared. Despite the harsh conditions, the calf found a small pool. This small miracle was a reminder that even in a republic of dry earth, life persists. The mother bear watched over her young, a symbol of resilience. The drought would not last forever, and the canyon would blossom again. The discrimination between survival and despair was clear, but the peaceful scene offered a glimmer of hope.

中文梗概

干旱考验着自然的微妙平衡。在峡谷中，母熊和幼崽寻找水源，河床干涸，海湾炎热。一匹野马凝视着这片土地，与自然默默呼应。尽管条件恶劣，幼崽找到了小水洼，象征着生命的顽强。干旱终将过去，峡谷会再次花开。

delicate UK /delɪkət/ US /ˈdɛlɪkɪt/

adj. · 纤细的；易碎的；精致的；微妙的

用法：delicate 可形容物品的脆弱、人体的娇弱、问题的微妙，注意它作形容词，常与 balance、skin、issue 等搭配。

  • The delicate glass figurine shattered when it fell from the shelf.
    那个精致的玻璃小雕像从架子上掉下来，摔得粉碎。
  • Negotiating a peace treaty is a delicate process that requires patience and skill.
    谈判和平条约是一个微妙的过程，需要耐心和技巧。

drought /draʊt/

n. · 长期无雨导致的干旱灾害

用法：drought 是可数名词，常与 'a severe drought'、'the drought' 搭配，也可用作定语，如 'drought relief'（抗旱救济）。

  • The drought lasted for three months and destroyed most of the crops.
    这场干旱持续了三个月，毁掉了大部分庄稼。
  • Farmers are struggling to keep their cattle alive during the drought.
    干旱期间，农民们艰难地维持牲畜存活。

搭配：{'en': 'severe drought', 'zh': '严重干旱'}、{'en': 'drought relief', 'zh': '抗旱救济'}、{'en': 'drought-resistant crops', 'zh': '抗旱作物'}、{'en': 'end a drought', 'zh': '结束干旱'}、{'en': 'drought conditions', 'zh': '干旱状况'}

correspond UK /kɒrɪ'spɒnd/ US /,kɔrə'spɑnd/

v · 相符合；相一致；对应

用法：不及物动词，常与 with 或 to 搭配；也可表示“通信”，此时常与 with 连用。

  • The results correspond exactly to our predictions.
    结果和我们的预言完全一致。
  • We corresponded by email for over a year before we met.
    我们在见面之前用电子邮件通信了一年多。

搭配：{'en': 'correspond with', 'zh': '与…相符；与…通信'}、{'en': 'correspond to', 'zh': '相当于；对应于'}、{'en': 'correspond closely', 'zh': '紧密相符'}、{'en': 'corresponding period', 'zh': '同期'}、{'en': 'correspond exactly', 'zh': '完全一致'}

bay UK /beɪ/ US /be/

n. · 海湾（海洋或湖泊伸入陆地的部分）

用法：作名词时，常与 'the' 连用指特定海湾，如 'the Bay'（旧金山湾）；也可作动词，意为'（狗）吠叫'，但较少用。

  • The ship anchored in the bay to wait out the storm.
    船停泊在海湾里等待暴风雨过去。
  • They live in a small town on the bay, where they enjoy fishing every morning.
    他们住在海湾边的一个小镇上，每天早晨都喜欢钓鱼。

搭配：{'en': 'a bay area', 'zh': '海湾地区'}、{'en': 'bay window', 'zh': '凸窗'}、{'en': 'at bay', 'zh': '陷入绝境；被逼到绝路'}、{'en': 'keep something at bay', 'zh': '使……不能接近；遏制'}

beast UK /biːst/ US /bist/

n. · 野兽；牲畜

用法：beast 常用于指大型野生动物或家畜，也可比喻人（如‘a beast of a man’），在正式语境中较少用。

  • The beast roamed the forest at night, its eyes glowing in the dark.
    这头野兽在夜间游荡于森林，眼睛在黑暗中发光。
  • In the story, the hero had to fight a fierce beast to save the village.
    故事中，英雄必须与一头凶猛的野兽搏斗以拯救村庄。

搭配：{'en': 'wild beast', 'zh': '野兽'}、{'en': 'beast of burden', 'zh': '驮兽（如牛、马）'}、{'en': 'beast mode', 'zh': '野兽模式（指全力以赴的状态）'}、{'en': 'beastly weather', 'zh': '恶劣的天气'}

blossom UK /blɒs(ə)m/ US /blɑsəm/

n./v. · 花；开花（尤指果树或观赏植物的花）

用法：作名词时常用复数 blossoms 指“花朵”；作动词时表示“开花”，也可引申为“发展、成长”。

  • The cherry trees are in full blossom along the riverbank.
    河岸边的樱花树正盛开。
  • Her talent began to blossom after she joined the art club.
    加入艺术俱乐部后，她的才华开始绽放。

搭配：{'en': 'cherry blossom', 'zh': '樱花'}、{'en': 'in blossom', 'zh': '开花（状态）'}、{'en': 'blossom into', 'zh': '发展成'}、{'en': 'apple blossom', 'zh': '苹果花'}

calf

n. · 小牛；牛犊

用法：calf 的复数形式是 calves，注意 f 变 ves；也可指“小腿肚”，但本主题下指动物幼崽。

  • The farmer fed the newborn calf with a bottle of milk.
    农夫用一瓶牛奶喂养新生的小牛。
  • A mother cow will often lick her calf to clean it after birth.
    母牛在分娩后常常舔舐小牛以清洁它。

搭配：{'en': 'a newborn calf', 'zh': '新生小牛'}、{'en': 'calf milk', 'zh': '小牛喝的奶'}、{'en': 'calf pen', 'zh': '小牛栏'}、{'en': 'calf muscle', 'zh': '小腿肌肉'}

canyon

n. · 峡谷，通常指深而窄、两侧陡峭的河谷。

用法：可数名词，常与 the 连用特指某峡谷，如 the Grand Canyon。

  • The river carved a deep canyon over millions of years.
    这条河经过数百万年冲刷，切出了一道深深的峡谷。
  • We hiked to the bottom of the canyon and camped by the stream.
    我们徒步下到峡谷底部，在溪边露营。

搭配：{'en': 'the Grand Canyon', 'zh': '大峡谷'}、{'en': 'a deep canyon', 'zh': '深峡谷'}、{'en': 'canyon walls', 'zh': '峡谷壁'}、{'en': 'canyon floor', 'zh': '峡谷底部'}、{'en': 'carve a canyon', 'zh': '切出峡谷'}
