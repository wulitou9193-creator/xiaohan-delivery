语境短文

渔夫的智慧晚宴

A resourceful fisherman named Tom decided to cater a banquet for his village. He used fresh dairy and dough to make bread, and fried bacon for a smoky flavor. The cocktail he invented became a hit. However, a bacteria epidemic threatened the village's mental health. Tom's disease-fighting recipes helped everyone stay strong. The banquet was a success, proving that simple food can heal. Tom's resourcefulness saved the day. The villagers learned that cooking is not just about food, but about community and care.

中文梗概

一位机智的渔夫为村庄举办了一场宴会，用乳制品和面团制作面包，煎培根，还发明了鸡尾酒。一场细菌疫情威胁村民健康，但他的食谱帮助大家抵抗疾病，最终宴会成功，证明了简单食物也能治愈人心。

resourceful UK /rɪ'sɔːsfl/ US /rɪ'sɔrsfl/

adj · 机智的；善于随机应变的

用法：常用来形容人，指在困难情况下能巧妙利用现有条件解决问题，褒义词。

  • The resourceful chef turned leftover vegetables into a delicious soup.
    那位机智的厨师把剩菜变成了美味的汤。
  • She is a resourceful problem-solver who always finds a way out.
    她是个机智的问题解决者，总能找到出路。

fisherman UK /fɪʃəmən/ US /fɪʃɚmən/

n · 渔民，渔夫，以捕鱼为业的人

用法：可数名词，复数形式为 fishermen，不可写作 fishermans。通常指职业捕鱼者，也可泛指钓鱼的人。

  • The fisherman cast his net into the calm sea at dawn.
    黎明时分，渔夫把渔网撒向平静的海面。
  • Every fisherman knows that patience is the key to a good catch.
    每个渔夫都知道，耐心是捕捞丰收的关键。

搭配：{'en': "a fisherman's boat", 'zh': '渔夫的小船'}、{'en': 'commercial fishermen', 'zh': '商业渔民（以捕鱼为业）'}、{'en': "fisherman's tale", 'zh': '渔夫的故事（常指夸张的吹嘘）'}、{'en': 'an old fisherman', 'zh': '老渔翁'}、{'en': "fisherman's net", 'zh': '渔网'}

cater UK /keɪtə/ US /ketɚ/

v. · 承办酒席；迎合，满足（需求）

用法：作‘承办酒席’时常用cater for（英式）或cater to（美式）；作‘迎合’时常用cater to，常带贬义。

  • The restaurant can cater for up to 200 guests at a wedding.
    这家餐厅可以承办多达200人的婚宴。
  • Some TV shows only cater to the lowest common denominator.
    有些电视节目只迎合大众的最低品味。

搭配：{'en': 'cater for an event', 'zh': '为活动承办餐饮'}、{'en': "cater to someone's needs", 'zh': '迎合某人的需求'}、{'en': 'catering service', 'zh': '餐饮服务'}、{'en': 'cater to the masses', 'zh': '迎合大众'}、{'en': 'cater for all tastes', 'zh': '满足各种口味'}

cocktail

n. · 鸡尾酒（混合酒精饮料）

用法：cocktail 是可数名词，常与 make/mix 搭配，也可作定语（如 cocktail party）。

  • She ordered a classic martini cocktail at the bar.
    她在酒吧点了一杯经典马提尼鸡尾酒。
  • The host prepared a fruit cocktail for the guests.
    主人为客人准备了一份水果鸡尾酒。

搭配：{'en': 'cocktail party', 'zh': '鸡尾酒会'}、{'en': 'cocktail dress', 'zh': '鸡尾酒礼服'}、{'en': 'fruit cocktail', 'zh': '水果鸡尾酒（罐头水果）'}、{'en': 'cocktail shaker', 'zh': '调酒器'}、{'en': 'cocktail lounge', 'zh': '鸡尾酒廊'}

dairy UK /deərɪ/ US /dɛri/

n./adj. · 奶制品；奶制的

用法：作名词时通常不可数，指奶制品总称；作形容词时只用于名词前，如 dairy products。

  • I'm lactose intolerant, so I avoid all dairy products.
    我有乳糖不耐受，所以避免所有奶制品。
  • The dairy industry has been criticized for its environmental impact.
    奶制品行业因其环境影响而受到批评。

搭配：{'en': 'dairy products', 'zh': '奶制品'}、{'en': 'dairy farm', 'zh': '奶牛场'}、{'en': 'dairy cattle', 'zh': '奶牛'}、{'en': 'dairy-free', 'zh': '不含奶制品的'}、{'en': 'dairy aisle', 'zh': '奶制品区（超市）'}

dough

n. · 生面团；钱（俚语）

用法：作为不可数名词，表示“生面团”；在俚语中可表示“钱”，但较口语化。

  • She kneaded the dough for ten minutes until it was smooth and elastic.
    她揉搓了面团十分钟，直到它变得光滑且有弹性。
  • He's been saving up his dough to buy a new car.
    他一直在攒钱买新车。

搭配：{'en': 'knead the dough', 'zh': '揉面团'}、{'en': 'roll out the dough', 'zh': '擀面团'}、{'en': 'yeast dough', 'zh': '酵母面团'}、{'en': 'pizza dough', 'zh': '披萨面团'}、{'en': 'make good dough', 'zh': '赚大钱（俚语）'}

bacon UK /beɪkən/ US /bekən/

n. · 咸猪肉；熏猪肉（通常切薄片煎食）

用法：不可数名词，常用作早餐食材；短语 bring home the bacon 意为‘赚钱养家’。

  • I love the smell of bacon sizzling in the pan on a lazy Sunday morning.
    我喜欢懒散周日早晨煎锅里培根滋滋作响的香味。
  • She ordered a bacon cheeseburger with extra pickles for lunch.
    她午餐点了一个加额外酸黄瓜的培根芝士汉堡。

搭配：{'en': 'crispy bacon', 'zh': '脆培根'}、{'en': 'bacon and eggs', 'zh': '培根煎蛋'}、{'en': 'bacon sandwich', 'zh': '培根三明治'}、{'en': 'bacon fat', 'zh': '培根油'}、{'en': 'bring home the bacon', 'zh': '赚钱养家'}

banquet

n. · 宴会；盛宴

用法：通常指正式、隆重的宴会，常与 hold, attend, give 搭配。

  • The wedding banquet was held in a grand hotel with over 300 guests.
    婚宴在一家豪华酒店举行，有300多位宾客。
  • They prepared a lavish banquet to celebrate the company's 50th anniversary.
    他们准备了一场丰盛的宴会来庆祝公司成立50周年。
