语境短文

情感与关系：一次尴尬的约会

Last Friday, I went on a blind date arranged by a friend. The moment I saw her, I felt a wave of anxiety. She was beautiful, but I was so clumsy that I spilled coffee on her dress. The silence that followed was awkward and disturbing. I tried to apologize, but my words only increased her distress. She looked at me with despair, as if the evening was ruined. I felt a dread that I had made a poisonous mistake. My awareness of the situation only made things worse. To my surprise, she laughed. She said my backward coordination was actually charming. We ended up chartering a boat for a sunset ride. The evening turned out to be wonderful.

中文梗概

作者讲述了一次尴尬的相亲经历。一开始因为紧张和笨拙，他打翻了咖啡，导致气氛尴尬和令人不安。他感到绝望和恐惧，觉得自己犯了一个严重的错误。但对方却笑了，认为他的笨拙很可爱，最后他们一起租船看日落，结局很美好。

anxiety UK /æŋ'zaɪətɪ/ US /æŋ'zaɪəti/

n. · 焦虑；忧虑

用法：通常作不可数名词使用；表示‘对……的焦虑’时用 anxiety about/over，不接 to 动词。

  • She felt a lot of anxiety before her job interview.
    她在求职面试前感到非常焦虑。
  • His anxiety about the exam made it hard to fall asleep.
    他对考试的担忧让他难以入睡。

awkward UK /ɔːkwəd/ US /ɔkwɚd/

adj. · 尴尬的；笨拙的；不自在的

用法：常用于描述社交场合的尴尬或动作的不协调，也可形容物体使用不便。

  • There was an awkward silence after he made that joke.
    他讲完那个笑话后，出现了一阵尴尬的沉默。
  • She felt awkward in her new high heels at the party.
    她在派对上穿着新高跟鞋，感觉很不自在。

搭配：{'en': 'awkward situation', 'zh': '尴尬的局面'}、{'en': 'feel awkward', 'zh': '感到尴尬'}、{'en': 'awkward silence', 'zh': '尴尬的沉默'}、{'en': 'awkward moment', 'zh': '尴尬的时刻'}、{'en': 'awkward position', 'zh': '别扭的姿势'}

clumsy /klʌmzi/

adj. · 笨拙的；不灵巧的；不圆滑的

用法：既可形容人动作笨拙，也可形容言行不得体、缺乏技巧。

  • He made a clumsy attempt to apologize, only making things worse.
    他笨拙地试图道歉，结果反而让事情更糟。
  • The clumsy waiter spilled coffee all over the customer's new suit.
    那个笨手笨脚的服务员把咖啡洒了顾客一身，新西装全毁了。

搭配：{'en': 'clumsy attempt', 'zh': '笨拙的尝试'}、{'en': 'clumsy fingers', 'zh': '笨拙的手指'}、{'en': 'clumsy apology', 'zh': '笨拙的道歉'}、{'en': 'clumsy movement', 'zh': '笨拙的动作'}

despair UK /dɪ'speə/ US /dɪ'spɛr/

n. · 绝望，完全失去希望的状态。

用法：作名词时不可数，常与 in 搭配（in despair）；作动词时不及物，常与 of 搭配（despair of sth./doing sth.）。

  • She cried out in despair when she heard the news.
    她听到消息后绝望地哭了出来。
  • He never despaired of finding a cure for the disease.
    他从未对找到这种疾病的治疗方法感到绝望。

搭配：{'en': 'in despair', 'zh': '在绝望中'}、{'en': 'despair of', 'zh': '对……绝望'}、{'en': 'drive sb. to despair', 'zh': '使某人陷入绝望'}、{'en': 'a sense of despair', 'zh': '绝望感'}、{'en': 'despair over', 'zh': '对……感到绝望'}

distress UK /dɪ'stres/ US /dɪ'strɛs/

n./v. · （精神或身体上的）痛苦、悲痛、危难

用法：作名词时不可数，常与 in 搭配（in distress）；作动词时及物，后接宾语。

  • The ship sent out a distress signal when it started to sink.
    船开始下沉时发出了求救信号。
  • She was in obvious distress after hearing the bad news.
    听到那个坏消息后，她明显很痛苦。

搭配：{'en': 'in distress', 'zh': '处于痛苦/危难中'}、{'en': 'distress signal', 'zh': '求救信号'}、{'en': 'emotional distress', 'zh': '情感痛苦'}、{'en': 'cause distress', 'zh': '引起痛苦'}、{'en': 'distress call', 'zh': '求救电话'}

disturbing

adj. · 扰人的；引起不安的

用法：disturbing 通常修饰事物或事件，表示“令人不安的”，主语是物时常用；若修饰人，则常用 disturbed。

  • The disturbing images from the flood left everyone speechless.
    洪水那些令人不安的画面让所有人都说不出话来。
  • She found his habit of staring at her really disturbing.
    她发现他盯着她看的习惯真的很让人不安。

搭配：{'en': 'disturbing news', 'zh': '令人不安的消息'}、{'en': 'disturbing trend', 'zh': '令人担忧的趋势'}、{'en': 'deeply disturbing', 'zh': '极其令人不安的'}、{'en': 'disturbing thought', 'zh': '烦人的念头'}、{'en': 'disturbing evidence', 'zh': '令人不安的证据'}

dread UK /dred/ US /drɛd/

v./n. · 恐惧；害怕；担忧

用法：作动词时常用 dread doing sth. 或 dread to do sth.，表示'害怕做某事'；作名词时不可数，常与 a sense of 搭配。

  • I dread going to the dentist because of the pain.
    我害怕去看牙医，因为太疼了。
  • The thought of losing her job filled her with dread.
    一想到可能失业，她就充满了恐惧。

搭配：{'en': 'dread the thought of', 'zh': '害怕想到……'}、{'en': 'live in dread of', 'zh': '生活在……的恐惧中'}、{'en': 'a sense of dread', 'zh': '一种恐惧感'}、{'en': 'dread doing sth.', 'zh': '害怕做某事'}

poisonous UK /pɒɪzənəs/ US /pɔɪzənəs/

adj · 有毒的，有害的

用法：既可指物理上的有毒（如毒蛇、毒药），也可比喻有害的（如有毒的关系、言论）。

  • The snake's venom is highly poisonous and can kill a person within minutes.
    这条蛇的毒液毒性极强，几分钟内就能致人死亡。
  • Their relationship had become poisonous, filled with jealousy and resentment.
    他们的关系变得有毒，充满了嫉妒和怨恨。
