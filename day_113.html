主题：教育与学习 · 状态：complete · 打开学习单（新窗口）

语境短文

从受伤到独立学习

After a sudden injury, David had to stay in bed for weeks. His doctor prescribed a treatment, and he began to recover slowly. To pass the time, he decided to learn a new language online. He found an inexpensive course that suited his needs. David soon realized that he couldn't always depend on his teacher for help. He had to become independent. This sense of independence made him more confident. Every morning, he checked his temperature in centigrade to make sure he was healing. Then he tried to absorb as much vocabulary as possible. He started with the alphabet, memorizing letter by letter. He also created a classification system for new words, grouping them by topics. Thanks to these habits, he recovered faster and became an independent learner. He even stopped worrying about being overweight because studying kept him engaged. Now, he believes that true learning comes from within. He still appreciates guidance, but he knows how to stand on his own feet.

中文梗概

大卫因伤卧床，在恢复期间开始在线学外语。他找到便宜课程，逐渐学会独立学习，不再凡事依赖老师。他每天用摄氏度测体温，同时大量吸收词汇，并把单词分类。这种学习方式让他康复更快，也让他明白了真正学习的意义。

depend /dɪˈpend/

v. · 依靠；依赖；取决于

用法：不及物动词，必须搭配介词 on（depend on sb/sth），不能直接接宾语。

  • Children depend on their parents for food, shelter, and love.
    孩子们在食物、住所和爱方面都依赖父母。
  • Whether we can go on the picnic tomorrow depends on the weather.
    明天我们能不能去野餐，取决于天气。

搭配：depend on / upon、It (all) depends、depending on the situation、depend entirely on、a dependable person

independent /ˌɪndɪˈpendənt/

adj. · 独立的；自主的；不依赖他人的

用法：既可修饰人（如 an independent thinker 独立思考者），也可修饰国家、机构（如 an independent country 独立国家）；和 of 搭配成 independent of 表示'不依赖于'，这是高频考点。

  • She became financially independent at 22 by saving most of her salary and investing in index funds.
    她在22岁就实现了经济独立，靠攒下大部分工资并投资指数基金。
  • Teenagers in China are encouraged to think independently rather than just follow the crowd.
    中国的青少年被鼓励独立思考，而不是盲目随大流。

搭配：independent of、independent thinking、financially independent、independent study、an independent country

independence UK /ɪndɪ'pend(ə)ns/ US /,ɪndɪ'pɛndəns/

n · 独立；自主；自立（国家、个人的不依附状态）

用法：抽象名词不可数；最常搭配 from（independence from sb/sth）；指美国独立时，固定说法是 Independence Day 或 Declaration of Independence。

  • After three years of saving money, she finally achieved financial independence from her parents and rented her own apartment.
    攒了三年钱后，她终于实现了经济独立，不再依赖父母，自己租了一间公寓。
  • The American Declaration of Independence was signed in Philadelphia on July 4, 1776.
    美国的《独立宣言》于 1776 年 7 月 4 日在费城签署。

搭配：gain independence、declare independence、independence from (sb/sth)、financial independence、Independence Day

inexpensive UK /ɪnɪk'spensɪv; ɪnek-/ US /,ɪnɪk'spɛnsɪv/

adj · 不贵的，价格低廉的（含中性褒义）

用法：比 cheap 礼貌、得体，常用于形容性价比高或负担得起的东西，多用在商品、课程、教材等具体物件上；注意比较级用 less expensive 而不是 *more inexpensive。

  • The university bookstore offers inexpensive used textbooks for first-year students.
    这所大学的书店给大一学生提供价格亲民的二手教材。
  • She signed up for an inexpensive online course to improve her spoken English before the exam.
    她报名了一个价格不贵的线上课程，想在考试前提升一下英语口语。

搭配：inexpensive option、relatively inexpensive、inexpensive and practical、inexpensive meal、inexpensive textbook

absorb UK /əb'sɔ:b/ US /æbˈsɔrb; æbˈzɔrb; əbˈsɔrb/

v. · 吸收(液体/知识/信息);使全神贯注

用法：及物动词,直接接宾语;表"专心于"用 be absorbed in,介词是 in 不是 with。

  • Plants absorb carbon dioxide through tiny pores on their leaves during photosynthesis.
    植物在光合作用中,通过叶片上的小气孔吸收二氧化碳。
  • She was so absorbed in the novel that she didn't hear the doorbell ring twice.
    她完全沉浸在小说里,连门铃响了两声都没听见。

搭配：absorb knowledge/information 吸收知识/信息、absorb nutrients 吸收养分、absorb shock 缓冲冲击、be absorbed in 全神贯注于、absorb the cost 承担费用

alphabet UK /ælfəbet/ US /ælfə'bɛt/

n. · 一种语言的全部字母按固定顺序排成的系统

用法：作单数名词使用，常搭配 the；引申义可表示「某领域的基础」，如 alphabet of law（法律入门）。

  • The English alphabet has 26 letters, from A to Z.
    英语字母表有 26 个字母，从 A 到 Z。
  • My daughter learned the alphabet by singing an ABC song every night before bed.
    我女儿每天晚上睡前唱 ABC 歌，把整个字母表都学会了。

搭配：the English/Roman alphabet、learn/master the alphabet、in alphabetical order、the alphabet of something、alphabet soup

centigrade UK /sentɪgreɪd/ US /sɛntɪɡred/

adj. · 摄氏的（温度单位）

用法：centigrade 是形容词，必须搭配 degrees 或 scale 使用，单说「30 centigrade」是错的，要说「30 degrees centigrade」或缩写「30°C」。

  • Water freezes at zero degrees centigrade and boils at one hundred degrees centigrade at sea level.
    水在海平面的冰点是 0 摄氏度，沸点是 100 摄氏度。
  • Last winter, the temperature in my hometown dropped to minus twenty degrees centigrade overnight.
    去年冬天，我老家的气温一夜之间跌到了零下 20 摄氏度。

搭配：degrees centigrade、centigrade scale、in centigrade、30 degrees centigrade、centigrade thermometer

classification UK /,klæsɪfɪ'keɪʃ(ə)n/ US /,klæsɪfɪ'keʃən/

n · 分类；类别；分级

用法：作不可数名词时强调"分类的行为或体系"；作可数名词时指具体的"类别、等级"，尤其在"保密等级"语境中常作可数。

  • The classification of marine species by DNA evidence has changed what we thought we knew about ocean life.
    用 DNA 证据对海洋物种进行的分类，已经改变了我们对海洋生物的认知。
  • Employees are not allowed to share documents marked with a high security classification.
    员工不得分享标有高级别保密等级的文件。

搭配：classification system 分类系统、data classification 数据分类、security classification 保密等级、job classification 职位分级、classification of species 物种分类
