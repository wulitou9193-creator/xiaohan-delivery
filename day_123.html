语境短文

旅行与交通：一次意外的航行

The advancement of modern transport has made travel easier, but it also brings challenges. Yesterday, I made a reservation for a ferry trip to a nearby island. However, I had to restrain my excitement because my friend, a caregiver for her elderly mother, couldn't join us. She needed to stay home to help her mother recover from a minor injury. Aboard the ferry, I noticed a sign: 'Please restrain your pets.' I also saw a carrier for bicycles and a large compass on the deck. The crew reminded us to keep our luggage in the designated area to avoid any restraint on movement. The journey was smooth, and I felt a sense of freedom. After the trip, I reflected on how travel can be a treatment for stress, but it's important to consider the needs of others. My friend's commitment to her mother was a reminder that not all journeys are physical. She will join us next time, once her mother fully recovers. The experience taught me that even with careful planning, we must remain flexible and considerate.

中文梗概

作者讲述了一次乘船旅行的经历，强调了预订和克制的重要性。朋友因照顾母亲而无法同行，但作者在船上体验了自由与反思。最终，作者意识到旅行不仅是治疗，还需考虑他人。

advancement UK /əd'vɑːnsm(ə)nt/ US /əd'vænsmənt/

n · 进步，提升；晋升

用法：常用作不可数名词，指抽象意义上的进步；也可指具体的晋升，如 'career advancement'。

  • The advancement of technology has made travel faster and more convenient.
    技术的进步使旅行变得更快、更方便。
  • She was praised for her rapid advancement in the company.
    她因在公司中的快速晋升而受到赞扬。

reservation UK /ˌrezə'veɪʃn/ US /,rɛzɚ'veʃən/

n · 预订；保留

用法：常用搭配 make a reservation，表示“做预订”；也可指“保留意见”，如 have reservations about sth.

  • I'd like to make a reservation for two at 7 p.m.
    我想预订晚上七点两个人的座位。
  • The hotel confirmed my reservation by email.
    酒店通过邮件确认了我的预订。

restrain UK /rɪ'streɪn/ US /rɪ'stren/

v · 抑制，遏制；限制

用法：restrain 是及物动词，常接反身代词或宾语，表示“抑制自己/某人/某物”，后接 from doing sth.。

  • She couldn't restrain her excitement when she saw the Eiffel Tower.
    当她看到埃菲尔铁塔时，她无法抑制自己的兴奋。
  • The police had to restrain the crowd from entering the restricted area.
    警察不得不阻止人群进入限制区域。

restraint UK /rɪ'streɪnt/ US /rɪ'strent/

n · 抑制；遏制；克制

用法：常用作不可数名词，表示“克制、抑制”，也可指“约束措施”或“安全带”等具体装置。

  • He showed great restraint in not responding to the insult.
    他表现出极大的克制，没有回应那句侮辱。
  • The government imposed restraints on international travel during the pandemic.
    疫情期间，政府对国际旅行施加了限制。

caregiver UK /keəgɪvə/ US /ˈkerɡɪvər/

n · 照料者，看护者（指照顾病人、老人或儿童的人）

用法：常用作可数名词，指人，常与 'for' 搭配，如 'caregiver for the elderly'。

  • My mother is the primary caregiver for my grandmother, who has Alzheimer's disease.
    我妈妈是奶奶的主要照料者，奶奶患有阿尔茨海默症。

aboard UK /ə'bɔːd/ US /ə'bɔrd/

prep./adv. · 在船、飞机、火车等交通工具上

用法：作介词时后接交通工具名词，如 aboard a ship；作副词时单独使用，如 come aboard。

  • The passengers were all aboard the plane before takeoff.
    起飞前，所有乘客都已登机。
  • We finally got aboard the train just as it was about to leave.
    我们终于在火车即将开动时上了车。

carrier UK /kærɪə/ US /kærɪɚ/

n. · 携带者；运输工具

用法：作名词，常指运输工具（如航空母舰、运输机）或携带疾病/基因的人。

  • The airline is the largest carrier in the region, flying to over 50 destinations.
    这家航空公司是该地区最大的承运商，飞往50多个目的地。
  • Mosquitoes are carriers of malaria, a serious disease.
    蚊子是疟疾的携带者，这是一种严重的疾病。
